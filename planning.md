Purpose of Website | What is the purpose and goal of the website?| The purpose of the website is to inform students about school clubs, events, and resources. It will help promote activities such as suicide prevention events, study clubs, and accessibility resources for students with disabilities.
 |
Target Audience | Describe the target audience (age, gender, demographics) |The target audience is for college students of all genders and backgrounds, including students with disabilities who are  looking for activities, support, and school resources.

Graphics | What graphics will you use on the website? | we will use school- related images, mental health awareness graphics and accessibility symbols.

Color | What colors will you use within the site to enhance the purpose and brand? |We will use blue, white, and other calm and welcoming colors. It will have enough contrast so the website is easy to read.

Accessibility | How will the website accommodate people with disabilities? | The website will include readable fonts, high color contrast, alternative text for images, clear navigation, and content that can be used with screen readers.
